01 — The Big Picture✦

Healthcare isn't one journey. People move between different needs: finding medicine, seeking medical guidance, arranging care at home, buying healthcare products, and visiting a clinic.

The goal of the redesign was to bring these journeys together without making the product feel complicated.

5 services.
1 ecosystem.
A shared product foundation with service-specific experiences.
02 — The Challenge✦

Each service has a different user intent, workflow and decision-making process. The design challenge was not simply to redesign screens; it was to create a consistent experience across fundamentally different healthcare journeys.

12 — The Connected Experience✦

The strength of the redesign is not any single service. It is how the services can work together around a user's evolving healthcare needs.

Healthcare Products can support the broader journey alongside these services. The product therefore behaves less like a collection of features and more like a connected healthcare ecosystem.

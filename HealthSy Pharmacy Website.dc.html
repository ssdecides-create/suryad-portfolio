01 — The Context✦

From physical stores to a digital storefront.

HealthSy Pharmacy operates physical pharmacy stores. The website was created as a dedicated digital presence for the pharmacy business — not as a replica of the HealthSy consumer app.

The objective was to translate the familiarity and trust of a physical pharmacy into a modern web experience that helps people discover the brand, explore pharmacy information, find stores and engage with the business digitally.

02 — The Challenge✦

How do you bring a physical pharmacy brand online?

Customers traditionally interact with the pharmacy through its physical stores. The website needed to strengthen that relationship by making the pharmacy easier to discover and understand online.

04 — Designing the Experience✦

One website. Multiple pharmacy touchpoints.

The experience was structured around a simple progression:

01
Discover
Help users understand HealthSy Pharmacy.
02
Explore
Let users browse pharmacy information, stores, content and relevant offerings.
03
Engage
Provide clear opportunities to interact with the pharmacy brand and move toward the next action.

This keeps the website focused on the pharmacy business rather than turning it into another version of the HealthSy app.

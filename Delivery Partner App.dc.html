Overview ✦

The Delivery Partner App helps delivery partners move orders from pharmacy pickup to customer handoff with clear actions, timely information, and fewer decisions to make along the way.

Turn a complex operational workflow — orders, locations, deadlines, payments, verification, and exceptions — into an experience that feels simple when delivery partners are on the move.

Problem Statement ✦

A delivery partner doesn't just deliver an order — they manage a chain of decisions.

From accepting an order to pickup, navigation, payments, verification, and handling exceptions, every delivery involves multiple steps. The challenge was to simplify this complexity and help delivery partners stay informed and focused on the next action.

01
Too many decisions
Partners manage order details, pickup, navigation, customer communication, payment and verification across a delivery.
02
Time-sensitive deliveries
Normal and express orders have different timelines, and delays can impact the entire delivery journey.
03
Not every delivery is successful
Customers may be unavailable, refuse an order, request rescheduling, or require the package to be returned.
04
Handoff is more than a single action
Depending on the order, partners may need to collect payment, verify an OTP, complete an open-box check, capture information, or return the package.

Reflection & Learnings ✦
Key Challenges

Designing for partners who use the app on the move, often one-handed and under time pressure. Every screen had to balance a lot of operational detail (orders, addresses, deadlines, payment and verification) without slowing them down. Exceptions such as failed deliveries, refusals and returns needed clear paths that did not clutter the everyday flow.

What I Learned

In operational products, clarity beats features. Building the experience around the next action, instead of around data, made each stage easier to follow. Mapping the real delivery workflow before designing screens surfaced edge cases early and kept the flows grounded in how partners work.

What I'd Improve

Run usability sessions with delivery partners in the field to validate the flows under real conditions. I would also explore smarter prioritisation for multiple orders, offline support for low-connectivity areas, and clearer earnings and performance insights.
